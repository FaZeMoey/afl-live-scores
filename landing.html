Frequently asked questions

Is this tool free?
Yes, completely free. No signup, no email required, no hidden fees. Just open the calculator and enter your details.
How accurate are the estimates?
The estimates use official 2025-26 Centrelink rates and income thresholds. However, actual payments depend on your individual circumstances. Always confirm with Services Australia on 136 150 before making financial decisions.
What is the NARWP waiting period?
The Newly Arrived Resident's Waiting Period (NARWP) is a 1-4 year waiting period that applies to most new visa holders before they can access certain Centrelink payments. FTB Part A has a 1-year NARWP, while Parental Leave Pay has a 2-year NARWP. Humanitarian visa holders (subclasses 200-204, 866) are exempt from NARWP. Our calculator automatically works out where you are in the waiting period.
Can I get Centrelink on a temporary visa?
It depends on the visa type. Partner visas (820/309) and regional provisional visas (491/494) can access family payments subject to NARWP. Most other temporary visas (student 500, visitor 600, working holiday 417/462) are not eligible. You may be eligible for Special Benefit in cases of severe financial hardship. Check your visa now.
Is my data stored?
No. All calculations happen in your browser. Nothing is sent to any server. Your information stays on your device.
Is this affiliated with Centrelink or Services Australia?
No. This is an independent tool built to help families understand their potential entitlements. It is not affiliated with Services Australia, Centrelink, or the Australian Government. For official information, visit servicesaustralia.gov.au/families or call 136 150.
How much is Family Tax Benefit Part A?
FTB Part A maximum rate is $227.36 per fortnight per child (2025-26). It reduces when combined family income exceeds $62,634/year and cuts off completely at $117,194. You also receive the FTB Part A supplement of $938.05 per child at the end of the financial year. Calculate your FTB now.
How much is Parental Leave Pay in 2025-26?
Parental Leave Pay is $882.75 per week for up to 20 weeks, totalling up to $17,655. To qualify, you must have worked at least 330 hours in 10 of the 13 months before birth (about 1 day per week). This includes part-time, casual, and self-employed work. Visa holders must also have served the 2-year NARWP. Check your eligibility.
