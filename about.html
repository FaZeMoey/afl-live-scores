About SettleAU

Our mission

We build free tools to help everyone navigate life in Australia — whether you just arrived on a visa or you've been here your whole life.

Government websites are confusing. Centrelink rules are complicated. Visa pathways are hard to understand. We believe everyone deserves clear, simple tools that give you answers in seconds — not hours of Googling.

What we cover

SettleAU is organised into five pillars:

  • Money & Tax — Tax calculators, Centrelink payment estimates, rent budgets, and payment calendars
  • Visas & Immigration — Visa pathway comparisons, NARWP countdowns, and immigration planning tools
  • Health & Medicare — Medicare eligibility, bulk billing doctor finders, and health insurance comparisons
  • Family & Kids — Baby cost planning, school finders, and childcare subsidy calculators
  • Settling In — First week checklists, suburb comparisons, and practical guides for newcomers

Important disclaimer

SettleAU provides general information only. Our tools are not a substitute for professional financial, medical, or migration advice.

We are not affiliated with the Australian Government, Services Australia, the Department of Home Affairs, or any government agency.

While we do our best to keep information accurate and up to date, rates and rules change. Always verify important decisions with the relevant government agency or a qualified professional.
