Frequently asked questions

How do I open a bank account in Australia?
If you arrive within 6 weeks, you only need your passport to open a bank account. After 6 weeks, you'll need additional ID (100 points). Most banks let you open an account online before you arrive. Popular banks include Commonwealth, ANZ, Westpac, and NAB.
How do I get a Tax File Number (TFN)?
Apply online at the ATO website if you're a permanent resident or citizen. If you're on a temporary visa, apply at an ATO office with your passport. Processing takes about 28 days. You need a TFN to work and to avoid higher tax withholding.
How do I enrol in Medicare?
Visit a Services Australia centre with your passport, visa evidence, and proof of arrival. If you're from an RHCA country, bring your national health card. You can also apply online through myGov. Processing takes about 4 weeks for the card.
What transport card do I need?
Each state has its own: Opal (NSW), Myki (VIC), Go Card (QLD), Metrocard (SA), SmartRider (WA). Buy one at train stations, newsagents, or online. Most offer concession fares for students and seniors.
How do I get a phone plan?
Major providers include Telstra, Optus, and Vodafone. Prepaid SIMs are available at airports, supermarkets, and phone shops. MVNOs like Boost, Aldi Mobile, and Woolworths Mobile are cheaper alternatives on the same networks.
