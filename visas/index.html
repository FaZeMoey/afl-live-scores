Frequently asked questions

What is the NARWP waiting period?
The Newly Arrived Resident's Waiting Period (NARWP) is a 1-4 year waiting period before new visa holders can access certain Centrelink payments. FTB Part A has a 1-year NARWP, while Parental Leave Pay has a 2-year NARWP.
How long does a partner visa take?
The 820/801 partner visa typically takes 12-24 months for the temporary stage and 2+ years for the permanent stage. Processing times vary based on case complexity and documentation.
Can I get Centrelink on a temporary visa?
It depends on the visa type. Partner visas (820/309) and regional provisional visas (491/494) can access family payments subject to NARWP. Most other temporary visas are not eligible.
What are the benefits of regional visas?
Regional visas (491/494) offer access to Centrelink family payments, a pathway to permanent residency, and potentially faster processing. You must live and work in a designated regional area.
Do I need health insurance on a visa?
Most temporary visa holders must maintain adequate health insurance. Some visa types (e.g., partner visas from RHCA countries) may access Medicare. Check your visa conditions for specific requirements.
